Why it starts with a weekend.

A report can tell you where you differ. Saying it out loud takes trust, and trust takes time with the right people. The weekend gives you that: founders who get it, facilitators who've done this before, and no investor in the room. Everything after it works because of it.

The Cadence Weekend. Three nights, built like a protocol.

Three nights, at most four founding pairs (8 founders) at your stage and a team of four experts: facilitators, a business psychologist and an experienced founder. Every session has a purpose and a method from the research, and your own report decides what you work on.

A method developed by researchers, not invented in a pitch deck.

The Cadence Method was developed with two researchers who specialise in founding teams. They designed the six check-up dimensions, the weekend protocol and the measures we report, and they review the method every year against the data.

Built on peer-reviewed work on team conflict,6 facilitated debriefs,5 psychological safety4 and habit formation.7

Your answers stay yours.

People are more honest when they answer privately and know exactly who will see it.9 Your investor never sees your answers, your report, or whether you took part.

Room 1

Your founding team

You and your co-founders, and nobody else unless you decide otherwise.

  • Your own answers
  • Your Team Report and 90-day plan
  • Every choice about what to share, and with whom
Room 2

Your Cadence coach

One matched coach, bound by Cadence's confidentiality protocol.

  • Your Team Report, to check it and support you
  • What you bring to sessions
  • Never reports to your investor
Room 3

Your investor

Patterns across the portfolio, never people.

  • Trends across groups of five or more teams
  • How many teams asked for support, if they opt in
  • Never names, answers, reports, or who took part
