03The Sprint

Six to eight weeks on one problem. A start, an end and a result.

  1. One goal and one number,in the founders' own words.
  2. An expert who has solved it,in one-to-one sessions.
  3. A small circle of foundersworking on the same problem.
  4. Five minutes a weekto say what happened and what comes next.
  5. A last week to measure:where the number stands, and what they keep doing.

A Sprint starts every month. A team asks for one itself, so you only pay for help that is wanted.
