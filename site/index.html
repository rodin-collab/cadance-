Not just coaching. One system.

Cadence has three parts that work together.

  • Cadence Intelligence

    runs the Check, the weekly check-ins and the short exercises founders do between sessions. It turns what founders say into the patterns you see, and it matches every problem to the right person.

  • Founder coaches

    work on what happens between the co-founders: how they decide, who does what, where they want to go.

  • The Shadow Leaders network

    brings about 80 senior experts who have built, scaled and sold companies, for what happens in the company itself.
