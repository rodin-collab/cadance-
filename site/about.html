What we believe

  1. Small things become big.

    Most founding teams don't break in a fight. They drift, and nobody notices until it shows in the numbers.

  2. Founders tell the truth only in private.

    So Cadence is independent: funds pay, founders own what they say.

  3. Finding the problem is half of it.

    The Check points to the problem. Experts who have been there help solve it.

  4. One problem at a time.

    A start, an end and a result beat a programme that never finishes.
