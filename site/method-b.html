The pattern, never the person.

The Cadence method

One problem at a time. From unspoken to solved.

The method is simple. We help founders say the real problem out loud, work on it with someone who has solved it, and measure whether it moved. Here is what that looks like with one example.

UnspokenSaidSeen togetherIn a SprintMeasuredOff the list

The example, in seven beats

Example

FindSolveKeep

  1. Before1 of 7

    The problem.

    “We have nine months of runway, and we don’t agree: raise or cut?” Two founders, both thinking it, neither saying it. It is not in the board deck.

    Founder oneThinking it
    Founder twoThinking it

    Not in the board deck

  2. Find2 of 7

    It gets said.

    Each founder does the Check, alone: fifteen minutes with our AI interviewer. Both name runway as their hardest thing. Nobody else reads their words.

    Founder one · hardest right nowRunway
    Founder two · hardest right nowRunway

    Nobody else reads their words

  3. Find3 of 7

    They see it together.

    With a yes from both, their answers appear side by side. They agree on hiring and on the product. They differ on runway. It is shown as a question to talk about, not a verdict.

    HiringAgree
    The productAgree
    RunwayDiffer

    A question to talk about, not a verdict

  4. Find4 of 7

    A person steps in.

    Thirty minutes, free. They leave with one step for this week, and a straight answer: this is worth a Sprint.

    A conversation30 minutes · free
    One step for this weekAgreed
    A straight answerWorth a Sprint

    They solve it.

    Six to eight weeks with a fractional CFO who has done this before. One goal: extend the runway. One number: months of runway. Five minutes a week to keep it moving.

    Nine months at the start. Ten in week three. The Sprint ends, and they write down what they keep doing.

Matched by a person who knows your problem. One session first; more only if it fits.

Four rules hold it together.

  1. Private by default.

    Funds see counts across five teams or more. Never a team, a founder or a word.

  2. AI asks, people judge.

    Our AI interviewer asks and listens. Every judgement comes from a person, with the founder’s yes.

  3. One problem at a time.

    One goal, one number, a start and an end. Then it is done.

  4. Measured at the start and the end.

    We promise the work, not the outcome.
