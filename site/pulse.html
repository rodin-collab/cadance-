    Set your Lens

    A 30-minute call. You pick the 2 or 3 things that matter most to your fund.

  2. Week 1

    Cadence invites

    A partner sends a short heads-up. Then we send the invites, not you.

  3. Weeks 1 to 3

    Founders answer

    About 15 minutes, alone, on any device. Each team gets a private Team Report.

  4. Week 4

    Your readout

    Presented in a 30-minute meeting. Patterns, what teams asked for, and options.

What you get

  • The six dimensions across your portfolio
  • Where co-founders see things differently (from 10 teams)
  • What founders asked support for, and if they would co-pay
  • Themes founders raised in their own words (shared by 5+ teams)
  • Next steps, including options outside Cadence

What you never see, by design

  • Who took part, or who did not
  • Any team or any founder result
  • Any group smaller than 5 teams
  • Anything a founder wrote in their own words

Enforced on our servers, not just promised. Your fund signs the same rules.

The six dimensions

What the Pulse measures.

Research-based measures, the same for every team. Your Lens changes how we read the results for you, never what founders are asked.

  • DirectionWhere the company is going, and why
  • RolesWho owns what, as the team grows
  • DecisionsHow big calls get made, and by whom
  • ConflictDisagreeing about the work, without friction between people
  • TrustSaying the hard thing to each other
  • LoadEnergy and pressure over the long run
