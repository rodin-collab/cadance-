What you get

A readout you can take to your partners.

  1. What your teams name as hardest, across the portfolio.
  2. How many teams would welcome support from you.
  3. How many asked for a Sprint on a problem.
  4. A 30-minute meeting where we walk you through it.
  5. A personal call for up to three of your teams.

How it runs

Four steps. We do the work.

  1. 1Kickoff call, 30 minutes.

    We agree when it runs and which teams you invite.

  2. 2You send one link.

    You pass it to your founders with a short message. You never hand us their email addresses.

  3. 3The Pulse runs for two weeks.

    Each founder does their Check when it suits them and gets their own result straight away.

  4. 4Your readout.

    When it closes, your Portfolio Pulse is ready and we go through it together.

About an hour of your time.

One kickoff call, one message to your founders, one readout meeting. No contract and no invoice.

The message to your founders

Trust starts with what you tell them.

We give you the text and you make it yours.

"We want to support you better, beyond the money. We have asked Cadence, an independent team, to help. The first step is a private Check for you and your co-founder. It takes about fifteen minutes and you get your own result straight away. We never see your answers. We only see patterns across the whole portfolio, from five teams up. It is entirely your choice."

Questions

Why is it free?

Because the Pulse is the best way to show what Cadence does. We would rather you see your own portfolio than read our brochure.

What if fewer than five teams finish?

Then there is no readout, because we never show small groups. We extend the two weeks once.

Do my founders have to take part?

No. Each founder decides, and you never see who did.

Do you need our founders' email addresses?

No. You send them the link. A founder gives us their own address when they start, and we use it only for their login and their reminders.

What does a Sprint cost?

We tell you on the call.
