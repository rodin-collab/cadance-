Our promises

  • We never show a fund a team's name, answers, goal or numbers.

  • Patterns only appear when five teams or more took part. Below that, a fund reads "fewer than 5".

  • A fund never sees who took part. It sees a range.

  • Cadence data is never used in investment decisions.

  • You can download or delete your data at any time.

  • Your data is stored in the EU.

What the technology does, and doesn't

Emma runs the conversation and gives your own words back. She never judges a person.

Your audio is deleted at once; only the words are kept. A person checks the first results.

Support, not treatment

Practical work on how you build the company. When it gets personal, a certified psychologist can work with you inside a Sprint. That is support for you as a founder: no diagnosis, no treatment and no mediation. If you need more, we refer you privately, and your fund never hears of it.

Straight answers

Founders ask

Will my investor see my answers?

No. Never your name, your answers, your goal or your numbers.

Will my investor know I took part?

No. They see how many teams did, as a range.

Will my co-founder see my answers?

Only what you both agree to share, and never your sentences.

Who do I meet in a Sprint?

Your expert and the founders in your circle. They know who you are because you meet them. They hear what you choose to tell them.

Can I delete my data?

Yes, any time.

Funds ask

Why can't we see individual teams?

Because founders would not answer honestly. That you cannot see them is what makes the picture true.

What do we see, then?

What your teams name as hardest, how many want help, and how many asked for, started and finished a Sprint.

How do we know it works?

We do not claim it yet. Each Sprint ends with the number the team set itself, and founders can choose to tell you what it gave them.

Who runs it?

Cadence, end to end. You do not need a platform team.
