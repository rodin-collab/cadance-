The Cadence method

A way to make a founding team better, one problem at a time.

Investors name the team as an important factor when a startup fails. And team problems often start as company problems nobody solved.

Who has solved this before?

  • The Cadence Sprint. You choose one problem and work on it for six to eight weeks, with one goal and one number.

  • With people who have solved it. An expert one-to-one, and a circle of four or five founders with the same problem.

Open circles. One hour on one topic, for any founder. A good way to try it first.

  • Keep everything. Your library, and a report you can print.

Why it works

Built on what is known to work.

  • 8.1%

    Founders learn most from peers. In a randomised study of 2,820 young firms, owners who met in small peer groups every month for a year grew revenue 8.1% more than those who did not.

  • 28%

    Whose advice you get matters. In a field experiment with 100 high-growth startups, founders advised by peers with a structured way of managing people grew 28% larger.

  • 92%

    The team decides the outcome. 92% of VCs name the team as an important factor when an investment fails.

Sources: Cai & Szeidl, Quarterly Journal of Economics (2018); Chatterji, Delecourt, Hasan & Koning, Strategic Management Journal (2019); Gompers et al., Journal of Financial Economics (885 VCs).
